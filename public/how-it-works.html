How Brook Dell works

Brook Dell keeps one short list and shows it to everyone who visits. Add an item once and it appears on the page the next time anyone loads it.

  1. 1. Add an item. Write a short title for something that needs doing.
  2. 2. We store it. Items are saved in a shared project, newest first.
  3. 3. The page shows five. The five most recent items appear on the home page, with a tick beside finished ones.

Items are stored in a shared project, so the list can change between visits.
